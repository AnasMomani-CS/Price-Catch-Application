# Price Catch 🎯
**The Ultimate Local Marketplace & Price Comparison Solution**

Price Catch is a robust cross-platform mobile application designed to bridge the gap between local retailers and consumers. It leverages geolocation to provide users with real-time access to the best deals nearby, while providing sellers with powerful analytics to track their business growth.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🛒 For Users (The Buyer Experience)
* **Geolocation-Based Discovery:** Explore local shops in your vicinity through an intuitive interface with proximity-based shop cards.
* **Dual-Criteria Smart Search:** A sophisticated search engine that sorts products by both **Price (Ascending)** and **Proximity**, ensuring users find the cheapest and closest options first.
* **Top Catches (Hot Deals):** A dedicated section for high-discount products, automatically sorted by discount percentage (Descending) to highlight the best value.
* **My Catches (Watchlist):** A personalized tracking system where users can monitor specific products and receive alerts for price drops.
* **Instant Navigation:** Seamless integration with Google Maps to direct users to their selected store or product location.

### 📊 For Sellers (The Business Logic)
* **Analytics Dashboard:** Track real-time performance through two main metrics:
    * **Total Views:** Monitor how many times the shop or products were seen.
    * **Store Visits:** Measures actual conversion by tracking users who clicked "Go to Store" or "Catch Price" (Redirection to Google Maps).
* **Growth Tracking:** Built-in business logic that calculates the **Growth %** based on conversion rates from views to physical visits.
* **Inventory & Offer Management:** A full suite for Sellers to Add, Update, or Delete products and offers.
* **Dynamic Store Status:** Real-time control over store availability (Open, Busy, or Closed), reflecting instantly on the customer's feed.

---

## 🛠 Technical Stack

* **Frontend:** Flutter & Dart (Clean Architecture)
* **Backend:** Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore)
* **Local Storage:** SQLite

---

## 🧠 Business Logic Implementation

One of the core strengths of Price Catch is its data-driven approach. The application doesn't just display products; it tracks the **User-to-Store conversion funnel**. 

By monitoring the interaction between product discovery and physical store navigation (via Google Maps triggers), the system provides sellers with actionable insights into their market reach and customer intent.
